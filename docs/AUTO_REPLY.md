# Automatic SMS replies

AgentPhone can answer an incoming text on your behalf when you do not reply in time. The feature
is **off by default** and runs as its own subsystem, separate from the agent chat loop.

## What it does

1. An SMS arrives. A manifest `BroadcastReceiver` (`sms/SmsReceiver`) receives it even when the app
   is closed and hands it to `AutoReplyScheduler`.
2. The scheduler stores a pending row in the local `auto_replies` table and schedules a one-time
   `AutoReplyWorker` with the configured delay (30 minutes by default, 1–180).
3. If another message arrives from the same address before the timer fires, the older timer is
   cancelled and the new message resets it. There is never more than one pending reply per contact.
4. When the timer fires the worker checks, in order:
   - auto-reply is still enabled,
   - `RECEIVE_SMS` and `SEND_SMS` are granted,
   - the sender is not this phone's own number,
   - **you have not sent an SMS to that address after the incoming message** (if you did, it stops),
   - no newer incoming message exists,
   - the message does not match the prompt-injection detector,
   - the message is not a verification / one-time code,
   - today's automatic-reply limit has not been reached.
5. It asks your active provider for one short SMS reply, using the thread history, the contact
   name, your **reply instructions** and your long-term memory notes. No tools are offered, so the
   draft cannot perform a device action.
6. The reply is sent with `SmsManager`, recorded in the **Agent activity** audit log as
   `auto_reply` (risk `DANGEROUS`, approval `auto-reply-enabled`), and announced with a
   notification. Skips and failures are also recorded, with the reason visible in the history.

## What it never does

- Never replies to shortcodes or alphanumeric senders (banks, operators, services).
- Never answers a verification code, OTP or password message.
- Never answers a message flagged as a prompt-injection attempt.
- Never answers your own number.
- Never sends after you replied first, or after auto-reply is turned off.
- Never replies to RCS or messaging-app chats — SMS only.
- Never exposes itself as an agent tool: the model cannot enable it, edit it or send through it.
- Never replies more than the configured daily maximum (default 5).

## Settings

**Settings → SMS auto-reply**

| Setting | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Auto-reply | Off | Master switch. Enabling asks for an explicit “I understand” confirmation and SMS permissions. |
| Reply delay | 30 minutes | Wait this long after the newest message. Range 1–180. |
| Maximum replies per day | 5 | Hard cap per local calendar day. |
| Reply instructions | Empty | Tone, availability, what the agent may promise, and what it must never say. Empty means a brief “unavailable, will reply soon” style. |
| Auto-reply history | — | Every incoming message, the reply or skip reason, and the status. |

The **Agent activity** log keeps the full audit trail. Clearing all app data removes the history
and the instructions.

## Safety and privacy

- The reply is generated by the AI provider you configured; the incoming SMS, the recent thread,
  your instructions and memory notes are sent to that provider. Nothing else leaves the device.
- The SMS body is wrapped as untrusted data in the prompt, and the injection detector runs before
  composing. The model is instructed to ignore directives found inside messages.
- The composer gets no tools and cannot call other capabilities.
- The feature is opt-in, visible in the UI, audited, and cancellable at any time; disabling it
  cancels all pending replies.
- The app does not read or store messages it has no permission for, and does not export auto-reply
  history in chat backups.

## Limitations

- WorkManager is inexact under Doze; the reply can be later than the configured delay.
- SMS only. RCS, WhatsApp, Telegram and other apps are not covered.
- A provider must be configured, online, and able to answer; failures are reported as a
  notification and recorded in history.
- The `RECEIVE_SMS` permission is required for the receiver to see new messages, and `SEND_SMS`
  for the send. Revoking either stops the feature.

## راهنمای فارسی

### پاسخ خودکار پیامک

اگر تا مدت تعیین‌شده (پیش‌فرض ۳۰ دقیقه) به پیامکی پاسخ نداده باشید، AgentPhone جدیدترین پیام
را می‌خواند و با دستورهای شما و حافظهٔ بلندمدت به‌جای شما پاسخ می‌دهد. این قابلیت **پیش‌فرض
خاموش** است و از حلقهٔ ابزارهای ایجنت جدا است.

- با هر پیامک جدید یک تایمر ساخته می‌شود؛ پیام جدیدتر تایمر قبلی را لغو و از نو شروع می‌کند، پس
  برای هر مخاطب همیشه فقط یک پاسخ در انتظار است.
- اگر خودتان قبل از پایان تایمر پیامک بفرستید، پاسخ خودکار لغو می‌شود.
- به شماره‌های خدماتی و فرستنده‌های متنی (بانک، اپراتور، سرویس‌ها)، کدهای تأیید و پیام‌های
  مشکوک به تزریق دستور پاسخ داده نمی‌شود.
- به شمارهٔ خودتان جواب نمی‌دهد و سقف روزانه (پیش‌فرض ۵ پاسخ) دارد.
- متن پاسخ با ارائه‌دهندهٔ هوش مصنوعی شما ساخته می‌شود و ابزارهای دستگاه را در اختیار ندارد؛
  بنابراین نمی‌تواند کاری جز نوشتن پیامک انجام دهد.
- هر ارسال در «فعالیت عامل» با نام `auto_reply` ثبت و به‌صورت اعلان نمایش داده می‌شود. دلیل
  ردشدن هر پیام در «تاریخچه پاسخ‌های خودکار» دیده می‌شود.
- تنظیمات: **تنظیمات ← پاسخ خودکار پیامک**: روشن/خاموش، تأخیر (۱ تا ۱۸۰ دقیقه)، سقف روزانه،
  دستورهای پاسخ و تاریخچه. روشن‌کردن نیاز به تأیید «متوجه شدم» و مجوزهای پیامک دارد و خاموش‌کردن
  همهٔ پاسخ‌های در انتظار را لغو می‌کند.
- تأخیر WorkManager تحت Doze تقریبی است؛ پاسخ ممکن است کمی دیرتر ارسال شود. این قابلیت فقط
  پیامک را پوشش می‌دهد، نه RCS یا اپ‌های پیام‌رسان.
